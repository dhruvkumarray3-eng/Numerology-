## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run a paid digital-goods service. Nothing more.

### 2.1 Telegram account information
When you start the Bot, Telegram provides your numeric **user ID**, and (when available) your **username**, **first name** and **language code**. We store your user ID as your account identifier. We never receive your Telegram password, login code, or two-step verification password.

### 2.2 Account and wallet data
We store your wallet balance, total amount deposited, discount tier, referral ID (if you were invited by another user), join date, ban status, and whether you accepted the Terms. This is required to sell you products and to prevent fraud.

### 2.3 Order and transaction data
When you buy something we record the order details needed to deliver it and to support you afterwards:

- **Deposits:** amount, payment method, status, date, and the transaction reference (UTR / TXN ID) used to verify a crypto or bank transfer.
- **Account orders:** the country, account category, price, the phone number delivered to you, and the one-time code (OTP) that was issued for that number so you can retrieve it later.
- **SMM orders:** the service ordered, the target link or username you supplied, the quantity, price, and the provider's order ID.
- **Panel / source-code purchases:** the item purchased, price, and delivery status.
- **Redeem codes:** the code used, the amount credited, and the date.

### 2.4 Technical and diagnostic logs
We keep operational logs containing your user ID, order status, error messages and timestamps. These logs are used to debug failures, detect abuse, and secure the service. They never contain authentication secrets or payment credentials.

### 2.5 Support messages
If you contact support, we see the messages you send. Please do not send passwords, session files, private keys, API keys, or card numbers — we will never ask for them.

## 3. Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect, request, store, or sell:

- Your contacts, private chats, photos, or files;
- Precise location data or device fingerprints.

## 4. How we use your information

We use the information described above only to:

1. Create and maintain your account and wallet;
2. Process deposits, verify payments, and credit your balance;
3. Deliver the digital products you purchase and let you retrieve them later;
4. Provide customer support and resolve disputes;
5. Detect and prevent fraud, chargebacks, multi-accounting and abuse;
6. Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions;
7. Improve reliability by diagnosing errors and outages.

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making that produces legal effects.

## 5. Legal bases for processing

Where data-protection law applies, we rely on: **performance of a contract** (delivering what you paid for), **legitimate interests** (fraud prevention, security, service reliability), **legal obligation** (record-keeping and lawful requests), and **consent** (where we explicitly ask for it).

## 6. Sharing with third parties

We share the minimum necessary data with the providers that make the service work. We never sell your personal data.

- **Telegram** — as the messaging platform, processes your messages and account data under its own Privacy Policy.
- **OTP / number providers** (e.g. TemporaSMS and fallback SMS providers) — receive the country/app selection needed to reserve a number. They do not receive your Telegram identity.
- **LZT Market** — receives the account-search parameters needed to source a Telegram account.
- **SMM providers** — receive the target link/username and quantity for the order you placed.
- **Temporary-mail provider** — receives only the request needed to create an inbox for you.
- **Payment and blockchain networks** — a crypto transfer is public by design; the receiving address and amount are visible on-chain.
- **Hosting and database providers** — store the Bot's database and logs on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
- **Authorities** — only where we are legally required to disclose, or where disclosure is necessary to investigate fraud or protect someone's safety.

## 7. Data retention

We keep account and order records for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards, because we must be able to handle disputes, refunds, and fraud investigations. Transaction records may be retained longer where accounting or legal rules require it. Diagnostic logs are rotated and deleted on a rolling basis. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

---

## 8. Data security

We protect your data with access controls, restricted administrative permissions, encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) to Telegram and to our providers, and the principle of least privilege for staff. Only authorised administrators can view order data, and every admin action is logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify affected users and, where required, the relevant authority.
